Workshops and tutorials

Materials from workshops I have given at PyData, PyCon, ODSC, and elsewhere. Each one has notebooks you can work through on your own. Workshops marked self-study are not offered live now, but the material is still useful.

Bayesian statistics

Where to start: if you are new to probability, start with Bite Size Bayes. If you know Python and want the core ideas quickly, start with Bayesian Statistics Made Simple. Then move on to the PyMC workshops for real-world models. The book that goes with all of them is Think Bayes.

  • Bite Size Bayes: an introduction to Bayesian statistics in short notebooks, with no previous knowledge of probability assumed
  • Bayesian Statistics Made Simple: Bayesian methods using grid algorithms, for people who know Python (PyCon 2012–2016, SciPy 2019) · video
  • Bayesian Decision Analysis with PyMC: Beyond A/B Testing: A/B testing, Thompson sampling, and hierarchical models with PyMC (PyData Global 2025) · video
  • Bayesian Regression for Survey Data with PyMC: logistic, categorical, and ordered logistic regression (ODSC East 2025)
  • Bayesian Decision Analysis: from Bayes's theorem to Bayesian bandits, with NumPy and Pandas (ODSC East and PyData Global 2022) · video self-study
  • Bayesian Methods for Clinical Trials: PyMC examples and the FDA draft guidance on Bayesian methods self-study
